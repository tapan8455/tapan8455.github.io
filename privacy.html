AURA IPTV Privacy Policy

Applies to the AURA IPTV website, the AURA IPTV Xbox and Windows app, and the desktop app. Last updated 6 November 2026.

AURA IPTV is a media player. It does not come with any channels or content: you add your own playlist or provider account. We do not run accounts, do not collect analytics, and do not show ads.

What stays on your device

  • Playlists and provider details you enter (server address, username, password, M3U links), your favourites, watch history and settings. These are stored only in the app's storage on your device. They are not sent to us.
  • Xbox app log: the Xbox app keeps a small diagnostic log (app start-up, phone-remote status, which remote buttons were pressed and the length of typed text, never the text itself) in its own storage on the console. It is never uploaded.

Who the app connects to

  • Your IPTV provider or playlist host: to load channel lists and play streams, the app connects to the servers you entered, sending the login details you gave it. Their handling of your data is covered by their own policies.
  • A proxy you configure (optional): if you set a "CORS proxy" in the app's settings, requests to your provider, including your login details, pass through that proxy. Only use a proxy you control.
  • Content delivery networks: the app loads its code, fonts and icons from GitHub Pages, cdnjs, jsDelivr, Google Fonts and Google (for the Cast feature). Like any website, these services receive your IP address and browser details when files are downloaded.

Phone remote (Xbox app)

The Xbox app can be controlled from a phone. While the app is open it accepts connections on your home network (port 8765), only from devices that have the private link shown in its QR code. Button presses and text typed on the phone go directly to the console over your local network; nothing is sent over the internet or to us.

Children

AURA IPTV is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Removing your data

Delete a playlist inside the app, or uninstall the app (or clear the site's data in your browser) to remove everything it stored.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/tapan8455/tapan8455.github.io/issues.
