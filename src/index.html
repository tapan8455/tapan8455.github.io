Settings, Legal & Data Control

Legal & Content Notice

AURA IPTV is an open-source media player application. It does not provide, host, link to, distribute, or license any media streams, television broadcasts, or playlist archives.

Users are strictly required to supply their own sources and verify they maintain the statutory rights and licenses to view their supplied media in their jurisdiction.

Privacy & Zero Telemetry

All account credentials, watch history, favorites, and playlists are persisted exclusively on this device inside your browser's local IndexedDB sandbox. Credentials are never transmitted to external analytics or centralized servers.

CORS / Insecure Stream Proxy

Routes HTTP and CORS-restricted streams through an edge proxy.

When enabled, target stream URLs pass through the configured edge proxy. Never enable this when using an untrusted or public third-party proxy.
